Chilli Con Carne

Home

Ingredients

  • 500g rice
  • 1kg minced beef
  • 2x 400g cans of tomatoes
  • 2x 400g cans of kidney beans
  • 100g sweetcorn
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 packets of chilli seasoning mix

Instructions

  1. wash rice and add to cooker with 750ml of water and start cooker
  2. dice onion and strain kidney beans
  3. preheat pan on medium, then add oil, and add onion to saute till translucent (approx. 5 mins)
  4. add beef and cook till brown, then add seasoning mix and stir
  5. add both cans of tomatoes and bring to boil
  6. add kidney beans and corn and mix then allow to simmer till rice is cooked
  7. enjoy!
